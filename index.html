    How the value is worked out

    The isopleths were digitised from the published Figure NA.1 and checked against it: the traced lines sit within about 1 km of the originals in Great Britain and about 6 km in the Irish inset.

    The value is interpolated between the two isopleths either side of the site, in proportion to the shortest distance to each: v = v1 + (v2 − v1) · d1 / (d1 + d2). The dashed lines on the map show those two distances. Isopleths are drawn every 0.5 m/s: labelled lines at whole values and unlabelled hairlines at the halves.

    Inside the 21.5 m/s ring the map gives no lower value, so 21.5 is returned. Beyond the 31 m/s line the value is held at 31. The Channel Islands take the single value of 24 m/s noted on the figure.

    A checking and screening tool. Verify design values against Figure NA.1 itself and then take vb,0 = vb,map · calt (Equation NA.1, calt from NA.2.5). Isopleths in the Irish Republic are shown on NA.1 for interpolation only. Coordinates are OS National Grid (OSGB36); latitude and longitude are converted with a Helmert transform, accurate to a few metres.
